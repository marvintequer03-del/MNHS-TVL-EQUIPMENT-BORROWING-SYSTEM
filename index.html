Roles and Responsibilities:
Website Administrator: Manages the website and monitors borrowing requests, approvals, inventory, and records. The administrator does not need to own or physically handle the equipment.
Teacher / Equipment Custodian: Takes care of the actual school equipment, verifies approved requests, releases equipment to students, and receives the equipment when it is returned.
Student: Submits borrowing requests, collects equipment after approval, and returns it on the expected return date.
